What BeatLab is

BeatLab is a beat studio for people who make beats. Patterns, a playlist, a piano roll you can play and a full mixer, laid out the way FL Studio taught your hands. It writes ideas with you, it runs on your own computer, and it will never ask you for a subscription.

01 · Playlist

Paint the song.

Numbered patterns go onto a free playlist as clips, right next to your audio. Draw, paint, delete, mute, slice and select, with snap, zoom and markers. Shift-drag a copy, right-click to delete.

  • DrawP
  • PaintB
  • DeleteD
  • MuteT
  • SliceC
  • SelectE

02 · Piano roll

Play it in.

Click a key and it plays. Stamp chords, lock to the key, quantize, legato, and shape velocity by drawing. Record from your computer keyboard or a MIDI controller, or just hum the tune: BeatLab writes it down in key and on the beat.

  • Hum it
  • Chord stamps
  • Scale snap
  • QuantizeCtrl Q

03 · The brain

Start from a spark.

Pick one of twelve styles and press Make a beat: drums, 808, chords and melody, written in a moment and kept in patterns you can edit. Switch on AI melody and AI drums and a small model runs right in your browser. No account, no bill.

  • 12 styles
  • Energy and complexity
  • AI melody
  • AI drums

04 · Mixer

Mix it properly.

Every channel gets an insert with pan, reverb and delay sends, and a chain of effects you can bypass and reorder, from EQ and compression to half-time and Tune. Then the mix doctor listens, scores it out of 100 and fixes what it hears.

  • 23 effects
  • Mix doctor
  • Sidechain duck
  • Bypass and reorder

05 · Master

Finish it.

A master chain with glue, width and a ceiling, a tilted spectrum and true LUFS meters with targets for Spotify and Apple Music. Then export WAV, MP3, stems or MIDI and carry on in FL Studio.

  • LUFS
  • WAV and MP3
  • Stems
  • MIDI

Questions

Good to know.

Is BeatLab really free?

Yes. No subscription, no account and no ads. The AI runs on your own computer, so there's no bill to pass on to you.

Does it replace FL Studio?

It works the way FL Studio works, so you can sketch, arrange and mix without relearning anything. When you want your FL plugins, export MIDI, stems or WAV and carry on in FL. BeatLab isn't made by or connected to Image-Line.

Do I need the internet?

Only to load the app the first time and, if you switch them on, the AI models. Your beats never leave your computer.

Where are my beats saved?

In your browser's storage on this computer, saved as you work, with named versions you can go back to. Save a project file any time to back a beat up or move it to another computer.

Can I use my own samples?

Yes. Drop a WAV or MP3 onto any channel or straight onto the playlist. If FL Studio is installed, BeatLab can play its sample packs too.

What do I need?

Windows and Chrome or Edge. A MIDI keyboard and a microphone work if you have them, and the computer keyboard plays notes if you don't.

Privacy

No accounts and no analytics. Your projects, recordings and settings stay in this browser on this computer. The app loads its code, fonts and, if you switch them on, AI models from public servers (jsDelivr, Google Fonts, Google Cloud Storage), and none of those requests carry your music.
